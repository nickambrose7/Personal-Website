## About Me

Hello, I'm Nick Ambrose, a software engineer at Moebius Solutions. I build backend-heavy web applications in Python, with a focus on secure systems, databases, and developer tooling. Most recently, I've worked on a U.S. Navy document/workflow management application (Flask, SQLAlchemy, PostgreSQL), where I've owned large database migrations, contributed to reusable internal frameworks, and implemented security-focused features like certificate-based authentication and access control. I also hold an active U.S. DoD Secret clearance.

I studied Computer Science at Cal Poly, San Luis Obispo. If you want the full details, you can check out my [resume](/files/resume.pdf), browse my [GitHub](https://github.com/nickambrose7), or connect with me on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/nick-ambrose-70a5101ba/).

Outside of work, I'm usually surfing, golfing, hiking, or planning the next trip. I'm always open to connecting with thoughtful engineers and teams. Feel free to reach out if you'd like to chat!
